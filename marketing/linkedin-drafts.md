## Post 1 — Cooperative fulfilment starts with fit

Cooperative fulfilment should begin with an honest question: is the operation a fit?

Not every product, parcel or order profile belongs in the same setup. Product fit is confirmed during qualification. Vareya is generally best suited to brands shipping 500 or more orders per month. From there, clients can agree customised SLAs with Vareya, within boundaries confirmed during qualification. Customer support is included at no additional charge.

That is the practical version of cooperation: define the boundaries, agree the service level and make the operating model clear before inventory moves.

If you are assessing European fulfilment, start with fit rather than a sales call.

## Post 3 — Why scans-first matters

Why start with a scan instead of a meeting?

Because fulfilment fit depends on concrete operating details: monthly order volume, product type, parcel dimensions, destinations and any customs, tax or specialist handling requirements. Product fit is confirmed during qualification. Suitable smaller parcels have combined dimensions below 900 mm and a maximum length of 600 mm.

A scan puts those inputs first. It helps separate what is already supported from what must be confirmed before a proposal is shaped.

Have customs, tax or specialist handling requirements? Include them in the fulfilment scan so Vareya can confirm which parts of the proposed setup can be supported.

Vareya will review your answers and send an initial fit response by email within one working day.

## EU Parcel Levy — concept 1 (statistieken)
