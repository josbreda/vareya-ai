## 1. SENDCLOUD — CORRECTED POSITIONING

**What Sendcloud is (VERIFIED):**

Sendcloud is primarily a shipping and delivery-management software platform. Official description: "Shipping Software for Ecommerce Businesses". It connects ecommerce systems to multiple carriers, and supports labels, tracking, returns, shipping rules and shipping intelligence.

Sources:
- https://www.sendcloud.com/ — "Shipping Software for Ecommerce Businesses" — accessed 10 Aug 2026 — CONFIDENCE: HIGH (official vendor site)
- https://www.softwaresuggest.com/sendcloud — third-party review: "enables more than 23,000 e-commerce businesses to save time, effort, and money on their most crucial process: shipping" — accessed 10 Aug 2026 — CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM (third-party)

**Sendcloud and fulfilment:**

Sendcloud also serves fulfilment companies and 3PLs as customers of its software. This is NOT the same as operating a fulfilment network itself. Do not merge these concepts.

**Approved distinction (use exactly):**

"Sendcloud's core proposition is software and carrier connectivity rather than operating the merchant's physical warehouse and fulfilment process."

**Corrections applied:**
- REMOVED: any categorical statement that Sendcloud has no relationship with fulfilment.
- ADDED: the fact that Sendcloud serves fulfilment companies and 3PLs among its customers.
- Any referenced fulfilment network: NOT VERIFIED as belonging to Sendcloud itself; treat as belonging to customers and partners until proven otherwise.
