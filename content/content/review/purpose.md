# Why this matters

Behind the day-to-day work of fulfilment, we're working toward something bigger: a fairer playing field in ecommerce.

When smaller webshops and new brands can't get access to the same logistics quality as the biggest names in the market, it gets harder for them to grow — and over time, that pushes more of the market toward a handful of large players. That's not good for brands, and it's not good for the people who ultimately pay the price: online shoppers, which is to say, all of us.

By giving growing ecommerce brands access to the same operational quality that large international brands take for granted, we're working to keep that playing field open — so new and ambitious webshops can compete on their product and their brand, not on logistics they can't yet afford to build themselves.
