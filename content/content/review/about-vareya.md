# About Vareya

Vareya is a practical, hands-on fulfilment partner — direct, transparent about how we work, and built around the needs of the brands and shoppers we serve, not the other way around.

We fulfil ecommerce orders from a warehouse in Breda, the Netherlands, shipping across Europe, the United Kingdom and a range of other international markets. Vareya is generally best suited to brands shipping 500 or more orders per month, working with Shopify and Amazon FBM sellers in cosmetics, supplements, phone cases, accessories and other smaller parcel products.

## How we work

Every decision starts with the end consumer — we think from the receiver's side and keep improving the journey from order to delivery. We treat every order as if it were our own, and we don't see the brands we work with as customers, but as partners: we grow together, solve problems together, and build long-term success together.

No client is the same, so we adapt our processes to the brand, not the other way around. Clear communication and clear agreements build trust — that's the standard we hold ourselves to, not a marketing line.

## What we offer today

- Shopify integration
- Amazon FBM fulfilment
- Returns handling
- A carrier network including DHL, PostNL, Asendia, FedEx and Royal Mail
- Cut-off times of up to 23:00, available by agreement

Product fit is confirmed during qualification.

## Where we're headed

We're working toward a future where every webshop — regardless of size or revenue — has access to the same logistics quality, technology and service as the biggest international brands. That's an ambition we're building towards, not a description of what every brand can access today.
