## Quick answer

Shopify integration is available with Vareya's ecommerce fulfilment service, operated from a warehouse in Breda, the Netherlands. Vareya ships Shopify orders across Europe and to other international markets, and is generally best suited to Shopify brands shipping 500 or more orders per month. Product fit is confirmed during qualification.

## Capability block

Shopify integration is available with Vareya's fulfilment service. Vareya uses ShipHero as its warehouse management system, fully integrated with Shopify. Specific integration details, such as store setup, data mapping and go-live timing, are confirmed directly with Vareya during onboarding, since they depend on each brand's individual Shopify configuration. Vareya's fulfilment rates are fixed and all-in per agreement, with no hidden costs beyond what the agreement sets out.

## Process block

Step one, qualification: confirm Shopify as your sales channel and share volume and product details.

Step two, onboarding: connect your Shopify store with Vareya.

Step three, inbound stock: send stock to the Breda warehouse.

Step four, fulfilment: Shopify orders are picked, packed and shipped.

Step five, returns: handled according to the process agreed with Vareya.

## Who this fits

Shopify brands generally shipping 500 or more orders per month, in cosmetics, supplements, phone cases, accessories or other smaller parcel products. Product fit is confirmed during qualification.

## International shipping

Shopify orders are shipped to Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, China, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom and United States. PostNL is the main carrier for shipments within the Netherlands; DHL, Asendia, FedEx and Royal Mail complete the wider carrier network.

## Returns

Returns handling is available. Contact Vareya to discuss the required returns process.
