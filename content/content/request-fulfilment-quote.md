# Request a fulfilment quote

## Intro

Share a few details about your ecommerce operation and Vareya will follow up with next steps. This is a request for a quote, not an instant calculation. Vareya reviews each request individually as part of confirming product fit. Quoted rates are fixed and all-in per agreement, with no hidden costs beyond what the agreement sets out.

## Fields

Name. Required. Validation: Please enter your name.

Company. Required. Validation: Please enter your company name.

Work email. Required. Validation: Please enter a valid work email address.

Phone number. Optional. Help text: Optional, only needed if you would prefer a call back.

Website. Required. Validation: Please enter your website address.

Monthly order volume. Required. Help text: An approximate figure is fine. Validation: Please enter your monthly order volume.

Number of SKUs. Required. Help text: An approximate figure is fine. Validation: Please enter your approximate number of SKUs.

Product category. Required. Validation: Please select a product category.

Target markets. Required, multi-select. Options: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, China, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States, Other destination. Validation: Please select at least one target market.

Desired start date. Optional. Help text: An approximate date is fine, this helps with planning, not a guaranteed onboarding date.
