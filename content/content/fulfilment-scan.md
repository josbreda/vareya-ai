# Check your EU fulfilment fit

## Explanation

Check whether your current volume, product category, sales channels and target markets may fit Vareya's fulfilment setup. This takes a few minutes, does not commit you to anything, and gives Vareya what is needed to confirm product fit.

## Estimated completion time

A few minutes.

## Trust statement

Your answers are used only to assess fit with Vareya's fulfilment setup and are not shared beyond that purpose.

## Steps

Step 1, Business and volume: tell us about your company and how many orders you ship per month.

Step 2, Products: tell us your product category and typical parcel size.

Step 3, Current operation: tell us how you sell and fulfil today.

Step 4, Target markets and timing: tell us where you want to ship and when you would like to start.

Step 5, Contact: leave your details so Vareya can follow up.

Monthly order volume. Required. Help text: An approximate figure is fine. Validation: Please enter your monthly order volume.

Number of SKUs. Required. Help text: An approximate figure is fine. Validation: Please enter your approximate number of SKUs.

### Step 2 — Products

Product category. Required. Help text: Choose the closest match, you can add detail later. Validation: Please select a product category.
