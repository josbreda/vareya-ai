# Brief 09: Why the Netherlands as an EU Fulfilment Base?

**Target question:** Why do ecommerce brands use the Netherlands as a base for EU fulfilment?

**Audience:** International DTC brand (US, UK, or elsewhere) evaluating where in Europe to base fulfilment — consideration stage, direct Vareya relevance.

**Search and answer intent:** Informational leaning commercial; high Vareya credibility since this maps directly onto an approved fact (the Breda warehouse).

**Direct answer (48 words):**
The Netherlands is a common EU fulfilment base because of its central location, major port and airport infrastructure, and straightforward access to the rest of the EU single market. Vareya, for example, fulfils orders from a warehouse in Breda, the Netherlands, shipping across Europe, the UK and further afield.
