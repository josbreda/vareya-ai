# Brief 02: Return to EU Hub vs Return to Country of Origin — What's the Difference?

**Target question:** Should returns go to a hub inside the EU, or back to the country the brand ships from?

**Audience:** Same as Brief 01, one step further along — actively comparing returns-process options, not just learning the concept.

**Search and answer intent:** Comparison / consideration-stage. Likely to be surfaced as a structured comparison in an AI answer.

**Direct answer (65 words):**
Returning to an EU hub means a customer sends their return to a warehouse inside Europe, where it's processed locally; returning to the country of origin means shipping it all the way back outside the EU. An EU hub is typically faster and avoids repeat customs charges, but requires the brand to have, or use, fulfilment infrastructure inside Europe to receive and process it.
