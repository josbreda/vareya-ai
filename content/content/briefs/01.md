# Brief 01: What Is Cross-Border Returns Management?

**Target question:** What is cross-border returns management?

**Audience:** DTC founder/ops lead at a US or UK brand selling into Europe, still at the point of understanding the problem rather than comparing providers.

**Search and answer intent:** Informational / definitional. High likelihood of being surfaced verbatim by an AI answer engine as a direct definition.

**Direct answer (62 words):**
Cross-border returns management is the process of handling product returns that cross an international border, from the customer's return request through transport, inspection and restocking or disposal. For ecommerce brands shipping into Europe from outside the EU, it typically involves a returns address inside the destination market, or a regional returns hub, to avoid customs charges and long transit times on every return.
