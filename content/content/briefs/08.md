# Brief 08: How to Calculate a Reorder Point

**Target question:** How do you calculate a reorder point for inventory?

**Audience:** Ops manager/founder with a concrete operational task, slightly more advanced than Brief 07's audience.

**Search and answer intent:** How-to / formula-based. Evergreen, high citation value — a common, precisely-answerable question.

**Direct answer (55 words):**
A reorder point is the stock level at which a business should place a new order to avoid running out before the next shipment arrives. It's typically calculated as (average daily sales × lead time in days) plus a safety-stock buffer for demand or supply variability. Getting it right depends on accurate sales and lead-time data.
