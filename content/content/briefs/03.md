# Brief 03: Shopify Fulfilment Apps vs a Dedicated European 3PL

**Target question:** Should a Shopify store use a fulfilment app or a dedicated 3PL for European fulfilment?

**Audience:** Shopify store owner/ops manager already fulfilling somehow, evaluating whether to switch models — decision-stage.

**Search and answer intent:** Comparison / decision-stage, high commercial proximity.

**Direct answer (58 words):**
Shopify fulfilment apps connect your store to automated software or a network of partner warehouses, usually with self-serve setup and standard workflows. A dedicated European 3PL is a single fulfilment partner managing your stock and orders directly, usually offering more tailored processes and direct human contact, at the cost of a more involved setup than a plug-in app.
