# Brief 12: What Is Amazon FBM and How Does It Differ From FBA?

**Target question:** What is Amazon FBM and how is it different from FBA?

**Audience:** Amazon sellers (often already selling via Shopify too) evaluating fulfilment models on Amazon specifically — informational to consideration stage.

**Search and answer intent:** Definitional / comparison. High AI-answer relevance — FBM vs FBA is a frequently and precisely asked question, and this cluster currently has no dedicated coverage of it.

**Direct answer (58 words):**
Amazon FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) means the seller stores, picks, packs and ships their own Amazon orders, either themselves or through their own fulfilment partner. Amazon FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon) means Amazon itself stores the inventory and handles picking, packing and shipping. FBM gives sellers more control over fulfilment; FBA hands that operational work to Amazon.
