# Brief 11: What Is Ecommerce Fulfilment and How Does It Work?

**Target question:** What is ecommerce fulfilment and how does it work?

**Audience:** Early-stage founder/ops, broadest and earliest-stage audience in this set of 12 — not yet segmenting by platform or geography.

**Search and answer intent:** Purely definitional. Lower direct buying intent, highest authority/citation value alongside Brief 05.

**Direct answer (48 words):**
Ecommerce fulfilment is the full process that happens after a customer places an online order: receiving and storing inventory, picking and packing individual orders, handing them to a carrier for delivery, and handling any returns. It can be managed in-house or outsourced to a third-party logistics (3PL) provider.
