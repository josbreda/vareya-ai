# Brief 07: What Is Inventory Management in Ecommerce?

**Target question:** What is inventory management in ecommerce?

**Audience:** Ops manager/founder with an operational stock problem, not necessarily yet considering outsourced fulfilment — earliest funnel stage of this set.

**Search and answer intent:** Purely definitional / informational. High search-volume opportunity per the (dated, to be revalidated) RankScope snapshot.

**Direct answer (53 words):**
Inventory management in ecommerce is the process of tracking, ordering and controlling stock levels across sales channels so a business has enough product to fulfil orders without overstocking. It typically includes monitoring stock counts, setting reorder points, forecasting demand, and reconciling inventory data between the warehouse and every platform a brand sells through.
