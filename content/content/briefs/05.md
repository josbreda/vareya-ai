# Brief 05: What Is Third-Party Logistics (3PL)?

**Target question:** What is third-party logistics (3PL) and how does it work?

**Audience:** Founder/ops manager still orienting on outsourcing fulfilment at all — earliest funnel stage in this set.

**Search and answer intent:** Purely definitional / informational. Primary candidate for becoming the cited, authoritative answer to "what is 3PL" (highest citation-value brief in this set of 12).

**Direct answer (50 words):**
Third-party logistics (3PL) is the outsourcing of a company's warehousing, order fulfilment and shipping to an external provider, instead of managing those operations in-house. A 3PL typically receives inventory, stores it, and then picks, packs and ships individual customer orders as they come in, often also handling returns.
