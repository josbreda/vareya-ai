# Brief 04: Setting Up a European Warehouse for a Shopify Store

**Target question:** How do I set up a European warehouse for my Shopify store?

**Audience:** Shopify store owner actively planning EU fulfilment, later-stage than Brief 03.

**Search and answer intent:** Informational leaning process/how-to; moderate-to-high commercial proximity.

**Direct answer (58 words):**
Setting up a European warehouse for a Shopify store typically means connecting your Shopify account to a fulfilment partner based in Europe, sending inventory to their warehouse, and letting orders sync automatically so the partner picks, packs and ships them. The process usually starts with a qualification step to confirm your product and volume are a good operational fit.
