# Brief 06: 3PL vs In-House Fulfilment — How to Decide

**Target question:** Should I outsource fulfilment to a 3PL, or keep it in-house?

**Audience:** Founder/ops manager actively weighing the outsourcing decision — consideration stage.

**Search and answer intent:** Comparison / decision-support. Likely to be surfaced as a structured pros/cons or decision-factor answer.

**Direct answer (58 words):**
In-house fulfilment means a business manages its own warehouse, staff and shipping; 3PL means outsourcing those functions to a specialist provider. The right choice usually depends on order volume, available capital for warehouse space and staff, how much operational control the business wants to keep, and whether growth is outpacing what an in-house setup can handle.
