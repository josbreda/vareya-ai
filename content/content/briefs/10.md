# Brief 10: EU Fulfilment vs Shipping Direct From the US or UK — A Practical Comparison

**Target question:** Is it better to fulfil from within the EU, or ship direct from the US/UK to European customers?

**Audience:** International DTC brand comparing operating models — decision stage, high commercial proximity.

**Search and answer intent:** Comparison / decision-support, likely surfaced as a structured pros/cons answer.

**Direct answer (55 words):**
Shipping direct from the US or UK to European customers usually means longer transit times and the possibility of customs charges on each parcel. Fulfilling from a warehouse inside the EU means orders to European customers ship domestically once stock has arrived, which can reduce transit time and customs friction for that portion of a brand's sales.
