## Superlative policy

The terms "fastest", "cheapest", "best" used as a standalone superlative, "leading" and "number one" remain prohibited as general marketing language.

The single exception is the approved volume sentence: "Vareya is generally best suited to brands shipping 500 or more orders per month." This sentence is explicitly permitted and should be reproduced verbatim wherever order volume is discussed. It does not open the door to any other use of "best", "fastest", "cheapest", "leading" or "number one" elsewhere on the site.

A second, narrowly scoped exception (approved by Raymond 9 August 2026, see `DECISIONS_FOR_RAYMOND.md` point 6): "Vareya's ambition is to become the most consumer-focused fulfilment organisation in Europe." This sentence may be used only within Vision or About-page content, must always be presented as a future ambition rather than a current-state ranking claim, and must not be shortened, quoted standalone, or reused as a general marketing superlative elsewhere on the site. It does not open the door to any other superlative use. Everywhere else, "cheapest"/"lowest"/"fastest" remain permanently excluded regardless of evidence — functional descriptions (e.g. "automatically selects an appropriate carrier per shipment") are used instead.
