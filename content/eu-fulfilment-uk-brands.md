Step one, qualification: share your current UK-based setup, EU order volume and product category.

Step two, onboarding: connect your sales channel and agree stock intake to Breda.

Step three, inbound stock: send stock from the UK, or an existing EU location, to Breda.

Step four, fulfilment: EU and other international orders are picked, packed and shipped from Breda.

Step five, returns: handled according to the process agreed with Vareya.
