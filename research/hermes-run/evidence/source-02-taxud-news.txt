From 1 July 2026, the EU will apply a temporary €3 customs duty per item on low-value consignments (up to €150) imported from outside the EU, abolishing the duty exemption applicable until 30 June 2026.

€3 temporary customs duty (from 1 July 2026): is a fixed duty applied per item (not per parcel) for consignments with a value up to €150.

Proposed Union handling fee (amount and date of application in autumn 2026 to be determined): is a fee and not a customs duty.

The duty applies per item in a consignment, based on tariff classification (not quantity).
