## 2. THREE CONTENT BRIEFS (FIRST PRIORITY)

### BRIEF 1: What information does a 3PL need to prepare a fulfilment quotation?

**Direct answer:** A quotation-ready profile contains: business identity and product type; order volume and destination mix; parcel dimensions and weights; storage requirement; software platform; and decision priorities. Anything less produces an estimate, not a quotation.

**Definitions:** quotation vs estimate; destination distribution; SKU; pallet equivalent.

**Comparison:** what self-fulfilment data vs 3PL data looks like.

**Step-by-step:** the five data groups mirroring Free Rate Scan steps.

**Decision table:** field → why it matters → what happens if missing.

**FAQ:** 5 questions (minimum volume, timing, estimate vs exact, storage, platform).

**Sources:** carrier docs, platform docs, Vareya Claims Register v1.2.

**Vareya boundary:** no price shown; product fit confirmed during qualification.

**CTA:** "Check your EU fulfilment fit" → /fulfilment-scan/

**Distribution:** see section 3. **Measurement:** impressions → clicks → scan starts → completes → quotation-ready.

### BRIEF 2: What determines ecommerce fulfilment and shipping costs?

**Direct answer:** Volume, parcel profile, destination mix, storage, platform integration and service-level requirements — in that order of impact.

**Definitions:** pickup, storage, pick-and-pack, last-mile, returns handling.

**Comparison:** rate components across models (per-order, per-item, subscription).

**Step-by-step:** how each cost driver compounds.

**Decision table:** driver → cost impact → data Vareya needs → common error.

**FAQ:** 6 questions. **Sources:** carrier rate publications, industry reports.

**Vareya boundary:** "fixed and all-in per agreement — no hidden costs beyond what the agreement sets out" (approved wording). No identical-rate or savings claim.

**CTA:** Free Rate Scan. **Distribution:** section 3. **Measurement:** same funnel as Brief 1.

### BRIEF 3: Shipping software, carrier contract or fulfilment partner — what is the difference?

**Direct answer:** Shipping software prints labels and rates. A carrier contract sets carrier pricing. A fulfilment partner stores, picks, packs and ships — and typically includes software access and negotiated rates. They solve different problems at different scales.

**Definitions:** shipping software (Sendcloud et al. — neutral, no attack); direct carrier contract; 3PL fulfilment.

**Comparison:** capabilities, typical costs, minimums, when each fits.

**Step-by-step:** decision path from order volume and operational capacity.

**Decision table:** criteria → software → carrier contract → fulfilment partner.

**FAQ:** 5 questions. **Sources:** platform docs, industry guides.

**Vareya boundary:** Vareya is a fulfilment partner — not a shipping-software vendor, not a carrier. No claim of "fully independent" or "complete objectivity".

**CTA:** Free Rate Scan. **Distribution:** section 3. **Measurement:** same funnel.
