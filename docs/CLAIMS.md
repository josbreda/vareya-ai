## Unconfirmed — do not claim available OR unavailable

Import support, VAT-related support, IOSS, DDP, temperature-controlled storage, certifications, batch management, expiry-date management, FEFO, FIFO, regulated-product handling. **Do not state these are available. Do not state they're unavailable either** — silence or the approved fallback line only: *"Have customs, tax or specialist handling requirements? Include them in the fulfilment scan so Vareya can confirm which parts of the proposed setup can be supported."* (Previously this file listed VAT/IOSS/DDP flatly under "Explicitly NOT Approved" alongside customs — that conflated "not approved as a positive claim" with "must be denied," which isn't the rule. Corrected 2026-08-21.)

## Superlatives — prohibited except two narrow, exact exceptions

"Fastest", "cheapest", "best" (standalone), "leading", "number one" are prohibited everywhere else, regardless of evidence — use functional descriptions instead (e.g. "automatically selects an appropriate carrier per shipment").

The two exceptions, both requiring exact wording, no shortening, no reuse elsewhere:
1. "Vareya is generally best suited to brands shipping 500 or more orders per month." — usable wherever volume is discussed.
2. "Vareya's ambition is to become the most consumer-focused fulfilment organisation in Europe." — Vision/About-page content only, must always read as a future ambition, never as a current-state ranking claim.
